## What to expect

Free route airspace (FRA) allows aircraft to fly direct routes without following fixed airways. Europe's FRA implementation is 94% complete — this session covers the final 6%:

- **Remaining constrained volumes** — where FRA is not yet available and why
- **Military coordination** — the bilateral agreements needed to unlock final FRA blocks
- **Measured fuel savings** — EUROCONTROL data on efficiency gains from implemented FRA
- **Night FRA vs. full-time FRA** — the case for removing remaining time restrictions
- **Connection with the North Atlantic** — how European FRA connects to oceanic track systems

## Key questions this session will address

**Which parts of European airspace are not yet free route?**
The remaining constrained volumes are primarily in south-eastern Europe (Bulgarian and Romanian airspace) and certain military-reserved blocks over Germany and France. The session will present a current FRA coverage map and the timeline for completing implementation.

**How much fuel has FRA saved in Europe?**
EUROCONTROL estimates that FRA implementation across Europe has saved approximately 280,000 tonnes of CO₂ per year compared to the fixed airways baseline. Full implementation of the remaining volumes would add a further 40,000 tonnes annually.
