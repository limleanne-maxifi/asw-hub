## What to expect

CANSO moderates a panel of ANSP leadership discussing:

- **Modernisation funding** under different fiscal models across regions
- **Capacity management strategies** where demand has exceeded available resources
- **Investment prioritisation** amid competing demands for ATM, cyber, sustainability, and workforce development
- **Cross-ANSP learnings** on what works operationally across diverse governance structures

## Why it matters

This session cuts across the structural differences that often divide ANSP discussions and focuses on shared operational imperatives. The candour between CEOs on sustainable modernisation models is rare and invaluable.
