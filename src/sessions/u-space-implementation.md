## What to expect

U-space is live in multiple European countries. This session moves beyond regulation into operations:

- **Live U-space volumes** in Europe — where they are, how large, what operations they support
- **USSP-ANSP interface** — the technical and procedural reality of coordination
- **Common Information Services** — the EUROCONTROL CIS architecture and its adoption status
- **Incident reports** from early operational experience
- **Cross-border U-space** — the coordination challenges when drones cross national boundaries

## Key questions this session will address

**How many live U-space volumes exist in Europe?**
As of Q1 2026, seventeen EU Member States have designated at least one U-space airspace volume. The largest operational deployments are in France, Spain, Germany, and Sweden. This session will present a consolidated operational map.

**How does a USSP interface with an ANSP in practice?**
The EU regulation mandates USSP-ANSP coordination but leaves the technical implementation to national implementation. The session will present the interface models deployed in Germany (DFS/Deutsche Telekom) and Spain (ENAIRE/Rohde & Schwarz) as case studies.

**What happens when a U-space drone enters controlled airspace?**
The boundary between U-space and controlled airspace remains operationally complex. This session will address the standard operating procedures developed by ENAIRE for managing transition cases.
