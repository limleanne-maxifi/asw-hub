## What to expect

The Welcome Ceremony opens the entire Airspace World 2026 conference. Attendees will hear from CANSO leadership on:

- Global ATM performance and operational status
- The strategic priorities shaping the conference agenda
- Remarks on modernisation, safety, and sustainability across the air navigation services sector

## Why it matters

This is the session that frames how the rest of the conference is read. The priorities articulated here set the tone for every subsequent session and panel discussion. Operational leaders attending ASW 2026 should treat this opening as essential context for the themes that follow.
