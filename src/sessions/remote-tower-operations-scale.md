## What to expect

Remote tower technology has been validated in operational environments at dozens of airports globally. This session examines what happens next:

- **Multi-remote tower centres** — operating 10+ airports from a single facility
- **Regulatory certification** under EASA's new remote tower framework
- **Controller acceptance** and human factors findings from operational deployments
- **Business case evolution** — where the economics work and where they don't
- **Integration with UTM** and the future of mixed-airspace digital towers

## Key questions this session will address

**Which airports are best suited to remote tower operations?**
Low-traffic regional and island airports show the strongest business case. The session will present operational data from LFV's multi-airport remote tower centre in Sweden, the first at-scale deployment in Europe.

**What are the human factors challenges at scale?**
Operating multiple airports simultaneously introduces new cognitive load profiles. Research from the SESAR-funded RemTower project will be presented on controller workload, situational awareness, and fatigue management.

**How does EASA certification work for remote towers?**
EASA published its remote tower framework under EU 2017/373. This session will clarify what ANSPs need to demonstrate for certification and where the most common delays in approval occur.
