## What this theme covers

Airspace capacity is the dominant operational story of the mid-2020s. Demand recovered faster than the workforce, and a sequence of events — Russian airspace closure, Red Sea overflight restrictions, weather, industrial action — exposed how thin the network's reserve capacity has become.

1. **Capacity declaration and rolling forecasts** — how ANSPs are signalling realistic capacity to airlines and the Network Manager, and the consequences of over-promising.
2. **Cross-border flexible use and contingency** — the bilateral arrangements that kept European traffic moving through 2024–25, and what's being formalised for the future.
3. **Resilience as a measurable capability** — moving beyond resilience as a slogan to specific, measurable, audited operational performance under stress.

## Why it matters now

Capacity shortfalls are now a board-level issue at ANSPs, regulators, and airline groups. ASW 2026 will feature the first public retrospectives on the 2024 and 2025 summer seasons — and on the structural decisions made (or deferred) since.
