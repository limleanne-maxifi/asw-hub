## What this theme covers

AI in ATC has spent five years in the demonstration phase. ASW 2026 sessions in this track cover the harder transition into operational use:

1. **AI-assisted decision support** — conflict detection, arrival management, and complexity prediction tools that have moved past trial into daily use, with measured performance data.
2. **The certification problem** — how EASA, FAA, and national authorities are approaching the certification of machine learning systems whose behaviour cannot be fully specified in advance.
3. **The human–AI relationship** — controller training, trust calibration, and the human factors research informing how AI tools are designed and deployed.

## Why it matters now

The first generation of AI tools certified for operational ATC use are entering service in 2025–26. ASW 2026 is where the operational performance data will be presented — and where the next generation of certification questions will be debated.
