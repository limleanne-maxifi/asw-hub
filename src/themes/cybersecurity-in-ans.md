## What this theme covers

As ATM systems move from closed, proprietary infrastructure to cloud-native, internet-connected platforms, the cybersecurity envelope expands dramatically. ASW 2026 sessions in this track cover:

1. **NIS2 compliance for ANSPs** — the EU Network and Information Security Directive 2 brings ATM into a stricter compliance regime. Sessions cover the operational implications, audit expectations, and the gap between regulatory text and operational reality.
2. **Supply chain risk in ATM** — most ATM systems integrate components from dozens of suppliers. How are ANSPs and integrators managing supply chain cyber risk in a regulated, safety-critical environment?
3. **Operational protocols for cyber events** — the playbooks ANSPs have rehearsed (and in some cases used) for handling cyber incidents, ransomware, and denial-of-service attempts against operational systems.

## Why it matters now

NIS2 transposition is now active across EU Member States. The first formal audits and the first publicly disclosed ATM cyber incidents are shaping the conversation. ASW 2026 is the first major industry event where post-incident learnings can be discussed under structured information-sharing protocols.
