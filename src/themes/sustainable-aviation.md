## What this theme covers

ATM has a small but meaningful contribution to make to aviation decarbonisation. Sessions in this track cover:

1. **Free-route airspace and trajectory optimisation** — the operational status of FRA across Europe, Asia-Pacific implementations, and the measured fuel savings versus published targets.
2. **Contrail avoidance** — how non-CO₂ effects are being incorporated into operational planning, the EUROCONTROL contrail forecast trials, and the policy questions around reroutings that increase fuel burn to reduce contrail formation.
3. **Continuous descent and climb operations** — what's preventing CDO/CCO from reaching 100% and how digital coordination can close the gap.

## Why it matters now

ICAO's CORSIA targets, the EU ETS, and the Single European Sky 2+ regulatory package have made ATM's emissions contribution measurable and reportable. ANSPs are now publishing annual environmental performance — and the gap between target and reality is the conversation at ASW 2026.
