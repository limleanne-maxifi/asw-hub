## What this theme covers

Unmanned traffic management has progressed from concept to early implementation. ASW 2026 sessions in this track focus on the operational reality of integration:

1. **U-space implementation in Europe** — the EU U-space regulation has been in force since January 2023. ASW 2026 sessions cover the first U-space airspace designations, the role of Common Information Service Providers (CISPs), and operational lessons from early adopters.
2. **BVLOS at scale** — beyond visual line of sight operations are scaling from one-off trials to routine commercial use. What changes for ANSPs when drone traffic counts rival GA in some regions?
3. **Counter-UAS in controlled airspace** — the operational protocols for handling unauthorised drone incursions near airports and the technology stack ANSPs and airports are deploying.

## Why it matters now

The 2024–25 period saw the first national-scale BVLOS authorisations and the first U-space airspace declarations under the EU framework. ASW 2026 is where ANSPs will compare notes on what's working and where the regulatory framework is hitting operational limits.
