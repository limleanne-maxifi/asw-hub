## What this theme covers

Digital ATM transformation is the largest sustained programme of change in air navigation services since radar replaced procedural control. ASW 2026 sessions under this theme cover three interlocking shifts:

1. **Cloud and virtualisation** — moving controller working positions, flight data processing, and surveillance fusion onto cloud-native infrastructure. Sessions feature ANSPs that have already cut over (NATS, LFV, NAV CANADA programmes) and those mid-migration.
2. **Remote and digital towers** — multi-aerodrome control from a single centre, sensor-driven situational awareness replacing the out-the-window view, and the certification path for fully digital tower operations.
3. **System integration and decommissioning** — the unglamorous half of digital transformation. How ANSPs are retiring 30-year-old systems while maintaining 99.999% availability.

## Why it matters now

Most digital ATM programmes launched between 2015 and 2020 are now at the cutover stage rather than the planning stage. ASW 2026 is the first event where operational data — not concept demonstrations — drives the conversation. Expect candid debriefs, not vendor pitches.
