## What this theme covers

The ATM workforce challenge is not abstract. Many ANSPs are operating below their authorised controller establishment, and training pipelines take three to five years to produce a qualified ACC controller. ASW 2026 sessions in this track cover:

1. **Recruitment, retention, and the controller pipeline** — ANSP-by-ANSP comparison of what's working: aptitude testing reform, accelerated training, modular licensing, and the role of full-flight simulation in shortening the pipeline.
2. **The role of the controller in an AI-augmented OPS room** — how digital and AI tools are changing the day-to-day work of the controller, the human factors evidence on workload and trust, and the implications for training curricula.
3. **Workforce planning to 2030 and beyond** — the demand forecast, the demographic curve, and the structural decisions ANSPs are taking now to meet 2030 capacity requirements.

## Why it matters now

The pipeline lead time means decisions taken (or deferred) in 2026 determine the controller workforce of 2030. ASW 2026 brings together ANSP HR leaders, training organisations, and unions in a way no other industry event does.
