## What this theme covers

The Single European Sky has been a project for over twenty years. ASW 2026 sessions in this track are unusually direct about what's worked and what hasn't:

1. **SES2+ regulatory package** — what the revised performance and charging regulation changes for ANSPs from RP4 onwards, and the implications for cross-border service provision.
2. **Functional Airspace Blocks (FABs) — what next?** — the formal status of FABs after years of underperformance, and the bilateral and multilateral cooperation arrangements that have emerged in their place.
3. **Network Manager evolution** — how the EUROCONTROL Network Manager function is changing under the revised regulation, and the operational implications for ANSPs and airspace users.

## Why it matters now

The summer 2024 disruption made it politically impossible to defer SES reform any further. ASW 2026 is the first major industry gathering since the revised regulatory text was finalised — and the first chance for ANSP CEOs, the European Commission, and EASA to discuss it together publicly.
