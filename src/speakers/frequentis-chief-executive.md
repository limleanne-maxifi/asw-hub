## Role and remit

Frequentis is an Austrian ATM technology company listed on the Vienna and Frankfurt stock exchanges. The CEO is responsible for:

- **ATM product portfolio** — voice communication systems, controller working positions, remote tower solutions, and cybersecurity products for ANSPs
- **Global customer base** — 500+ ANSP customers across more than 150 countries
- **Research and SESAR** — Frequentis is a major SESAR research consortium partner
- **Digital tower expansion** — Frequentis is a market leader in remote and digital tower technology

## About Frequentis

Frequentis is headquartered in Vienna, Austria and employs approximately 2,200 people globally. It is publicly listed (FREQ:VIE, FREQ:FRA) and generates approximately €400m annual revenue. The company's ATM division supplies communication and safety systems to ANSPs on every inhabited continent.
