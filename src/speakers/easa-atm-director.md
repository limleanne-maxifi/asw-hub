## Role and remit

EASA is the European Union's aviation safety regulator, responsible for certification, oversight, and rulemaking across all aviation domains including ATM. Key ATM regulatory areas:

- **Part-ATM/ANS** — the regulatory framework for ATM and air navigation service provision
- **Part-ATCO** — controller licensing, training organisation certification, and competency requirements
- **AI Roadmap** — EASA's framework for certifying AI applications in aviation, covering Levels 1–3
- **Remote tower certification** — the framework enabling certified remote tower operations
- **U-space regulation** — EASA's role in certifying U-space service providers

## About EASA

EASA is an EU agency headquartered in Cologne, Germany. It employs approximately 900 staff and oversees aviation safety across 32 European countries. EASA regulations are directly applicable in all EU Member States and have been adopted or referenced by many non-EU countries.
