## Role and remit

NATS is the UK's designated ANSP, providing en-route ATC across UK airspace and oceanic services over the North Atlantic. The Chief Executive is responsible for:

- **Operations** — en-route centres at Swanwick (Hampshire) and Prestwick (Scotland) plus oceanic operations at Shanwick
- **Technology investment** — the £1.3bn programme replacing core en-route systems
- **Commercial operations** — NATS provides ATC services to 15 UK airports and has international operations in Middle East and Asia
- **Regulatory relationship** — NATS is regulated by the Civil Aviation Authority under a licence framework

## About NATS

NATS is a public-private partnership: 49% owned by a consortium of UK airlines (Airline Group), 42% by the UK government, and 9% by NATS staff. It was the first ANSP in the world to be part-privatised (2001). NATS handles more than 2.5 million flights annually and provides ATC for approximately 30% of transatlantic traffic.
