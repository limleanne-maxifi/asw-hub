## Role and remit

Skyguide is Switzerland's designated ANSP, providing civil and military ATC services across Swiss airspace and to parts of French, German, and Italian airspace under delegation agreements. The CEO oversees:

- **Civil-military integration** — Skyguide operates integrated civil-military ATC across Swiss airspace
- **FABEC participation** — Switzerland's integration within the Functional Airspace Block Europe Central
- **Remote tower** — Skyguide operates one of Europe's most advanced remote tower programmes at Swiss regional airports
- **Cybersecurity** — Skyguide is regularly cited as a European benchmark for ANSP cybersecurity governance

## About Skyguide

Skyguide is a Swiss limited company owned by the Swiss Confederation (99.7%). It employs approximately 1,700 people, of whom around 580 are air traffic controllers. Skyguide handles approximately 1.1 million flights per year in Swiss-delegated airspace and generated CHF 393m revenue in 2024.
