## Role and remit

The CANSO Director General is the senior executive of the Civil Air Navigation Services Organisation, the global representative body for air navigation service providers. CANSO members manage approximately 90% of global air traffic.

The Director General role spans:

- Representing the global ATM industry to ICAO, the European Commission, and national civil aviation authorities
- Leading CANSO's policy positioning on capacity, performance, sustainability, and safety
- Convening the CANSO membership across regional and global executive committees
- Acting as the public spokesperson for CANSO at major industry events including Airspace World
