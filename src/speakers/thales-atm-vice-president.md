## Role and remit

Thales is a French technology group with a major ATM division supplying automation systems, radar processing, and approach control solutions globally. The VP ATM oversees:

- **EUROCAT and TopSky-ATC** — Thales' flagship ATM automation platforms
- **SESAR industrialisation** — Thales is a lead SESAR industry partner and system integrator
- **Digital tower** — Thales' ATC suite adapted for remote and digital tower operations
- **Asia-Pacific expansion** — major programme deliveries in Singapore, Australia, and India

## About Thales

Thales Group is a French multinational with €18bn annual revenue and 80,000 employees. Its ATM division supplies systems to approximately 100 ANSPs globally and is one of two dominant players in the European ATM automation market (alongside Leonardo/Selex). Thales has been a SESAR consortium partner since the programme's inception in 2007.
