## Role and remit

ENAV is Italy's designated ANSP, providing en-route and terminal ATC services across Italian airspace. ENAV is listed on the Milan Stock Exchange (ENAV:MI), making it one of only two publicly listed European ANSPs. The Director General oversees:

- **ATC operations** — en-route control across Italian FIR and TMA services at 45 airports
- **Technology programme** — ENAV's development of satellite navigation (PBN) and remote tower systems
- **Commercial ventures** — ENAV's international consulting and technology subsidiary, D-FLIGHT (U-space)
- **SES performance** — ENAV operates within BLUE MED FAB alongside DSNA, DGCA Greece, and MATS Malta

## About ENAV

ENAV is headquartered in Rome and employs approximately 4,200 people, including 1,800 air traffic controllers. It handles approximately 1.8 million flights per year and generated €1.0bn revenue in 2024. ENAV's D-FLIGHT joint venture is Italy's designated U-space service provider.
