## Role and remit

The EUROCONTROL Director General leads an organisation of 2,000+ staff that underpins European aviation infrastructure across 44 Member States. Key responsibilities include:

- **Network Manager** — coordinating European airspace flows, slot allocation, and pre-tactical demand management
- **SESAR support** — funding and coordinating deployment of the Single European Sky ATM Research programme
- **Safety oversight** — publishing the European Aviation Safety Plan and Performance Review
- **Capacity forecasting** — the annual STATFOR long-term forecast and summer capacity outlooks

## About EUROCONTROL

EUROCONTROL is an intergovernmental organisation with 44 Member States, headquartered in Brussels. It manages approximately 11 million flights per year through its Network Manager Operations Centre and provides centralised ATM services in Maastricht Upper Area Control (MUAC) for Belgian, Dutch, German, and Luxembourg upper airspace.
