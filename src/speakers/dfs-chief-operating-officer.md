## Role and remit

DFS Deutsche Flugsicherung is Germany's designated ANSP, providing en-route and approach ATC across German airspace. As COO, responsibilities include:

- **ATC operations** — en-route control from Karlsruhe and Langen centres, plus approach control at German international airports
- **MUAC co-ownership** — DFS is a co-owning ANSP of the EUROCONTROL Maastricht Upper Area Control Centre
- **FABEC** — DFS is the largest ANSP within the Functional Airspace Block Europe Central
- **AI deployment** — DFS has one of Europe's most advanced operational AI trial programmes in ATC

## About DFS

DFS Deutsche Flugsicherung is a private limited company wholly owned by the German government. It employs approximately 5,600 people, of whom around 2,200 are air traffic controllers. DFS handles approximately 3.3 million flights annually in German airspace and generated €1.1bn revenue in 2024. DFS is co-owner and founding member of the MUAC alongside EUROCONTROL, Belgocontrol (skeyes), LVNL, and Luxair.
