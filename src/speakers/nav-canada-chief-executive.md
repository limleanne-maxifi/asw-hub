## Role and remit

NAV CANADA is a private not-for-profit corporation providing ATC and related services across Canadian airspace. As CEO, responsibilities include:

- **Domestic and oceanic operations** — ATC across Canada and Gander Oceanic airspace (approximately 60% of North Atlantic traffic)
- **Technology leadership** — NAV CANADA developed ADS-B oceanic surveillance and CPDLC data link standards widely adopted globally
- **Financial management** — NAV CANADA is funded by aviation fees, not government appropriation; COVID created a significant financial restructuring challenge
- **International leadership** — NAV CANADA has been a technology exporter, with its systems in use at NATS, ENAV, and other major ANSPs

## About NAV CANADA

NAV CANADA was created in 1996 when Canada privatised its ANSP — one of the world's first ANSP privatisations. It is a not-for-profit corporation with revenues of approximately CAD 1.5bn (2024). NAV CANADA employs around 5,000 people including approximately 2,200 controllers and 1,000 meteorologists. Its Gander Oceanic Centre handles approximately 550,000 transatlantic flights annually.
